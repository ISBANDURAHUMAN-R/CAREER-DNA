Privacy Policy

What CAREER DNA collects, stores, and uses.

Last updated: September 20, 2026

01 — Overview

CAREER DNA respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the platform and how that information is handled.

02 — Information You Provide

Depending on how you use CAREER DNA, the platform may process information such as:

  • Answers provided during the career assessment.
  • Career preferences and assessment results.
  • Information you voluntarily provide through contact forms.
  • Feedback or messages you send to us.

03 — Assessment Data

Your assessment responses may be used to calculate your career pattern and generate compatible career recommendations.

If the application stores assessment information locally in your browser, that information remains associated with that browser unless you clear the stored application data.

04 — Local Storage

CAREER DNA may use browser local storage to remember information such as assessment progress, completed assessments, results, history, and achievements.

Local storage data can generally be removed by clearing the site's stored browser data.

05 — How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Generate career-pattern results.
  • Recommend relevant career paths.
  • Remember assessment progress and results.
  • Improve the platform and user experience.
  • Respond to user communications.

06 — Information Sharing

CAREER DNA does not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with third-party services only when necessary to provide a feature, comply with applicable law, or protect the security and integrity of the platform.

07 — Third-Party Services

If CAREER DNA uses external services such as analytics, hosting, forms, authentication, or external career resources, those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

08 — Security

Reasonable measures may be used to protect information handled by the platform. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

09 — Children's Privacy

CAREER DNA is intended for general career exploration. Users should provide only information that is appropriate for the service and should avoid submitting sensitive personal information unnecessarily.

10 — Your Choices

You can stop using CAREER DNA at any time. If assessment information is stored locally in your browser, you can remove it by clearing the site's local storage or browser data.

11 — Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when CAREER DNA's features or data practices change. The latest version will always display the applicable update date.
